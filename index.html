01 / Problem

Most AI/SaaS pages say what the product does, not why anyone should care now.

Three leaks show up again and again on YC, Product Hunt and recent launch pages. They are cheap to find and expensive to ignore.

LEAK 01

Vague hero

The page says what the product does, but not which expensive workflow it removes or who should care first.

LEAK 02

Weak proof

No before/after, demo screenshot, customer result or credible reason to believe the claim on the page.

LEAK 03

Generic CTA

"Book a demo" is fine, but the page should often push a more concrete action tied to the outcome you sell.
