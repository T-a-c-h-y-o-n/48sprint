The problem

Most AI/SaaS landing pages explain the product, but do not sell the pain.

The product may be strong, but the page often misses the buyer, the urgent workflow, the ROI proof and the exact reason to act now.

↯

Unclear buyer pain

The page says what the product does, but not which expensive workflow it removes or who should care first.

◎

Weak proof

There is no before/after, demo screenshot, customer result or credible reason to believe the claim.

→

Generic CTA

“Book a demo” is fine, but the page should often push a more concrete action tied to the outcome.
